# Wattlyst calculator formulas (single source of truth)

Status: drafted 2026-10-13, implemented in `engine/` 2026-10-13 (Phase 1). Every formula here must pass `calculator-test-cases.json` before a page ships.
All defaults below live in `data/defaults.json` (the engine reads them from there; this table documents them).
Engineer review required before launch (see engineer-reviewer.md). Units are SI unless stated.

## Global defaults (editable by user, shown on the page)
| Symbol | Meaning | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| V_1ph | Single-phase voltage | 230 V (PK), 120 V (US toggle) | NEPRA Performance Standards: 400/230 V, 50 Hz, ±5% |
| V_3ph | Three-phase line-to-line | 400 V | same |
| PF | Power factor (AC loads) | 0.8 for mixed/motor loads, 1.0 resistive (heater, iron, geyser) | industry convention |
| η_inv | Inverter/UPS efficiency | 0.85 (UPS/older), 0.90–0.93 (modern hybrid) | typical datasheets |
| DoD | Usable depth of discharge | lead-acid/tubular 0.5, LiFePO4 0.9 | battery life convention |
| η_chg | Charging efficiency | lead-acid 0.85, LiFePO4 0.95 | typical |
| margin | Sizing safety margin | 1.25 | continuous-load practice |
| yield | PV output kWh per kWp per day | from data/cities-pk.json (Global Solar Atlas): the sum of the twelve monthly PVOUT values ÷ 365, three decimals | Lahore 3.995, Karachi 4.652 ... (full audit 2026-10-15 WL-106: the one-bill D7 size and the twelve-bill N17 size then agree for a flat bill) |
| days_per_month | Days in a month, for every day ↔ month ↔ year conversion | 30.42 (365 ÷ 12, data/defaults.json) | an average calendar month, so a year is 12 × days_per_month = 365 days on every page, as the yield is a 365-day average (WL-106). ## A. Conversions
- DC: `I = P / V`; `P = V × I`
- AC single-phase: `I = P / (V × PF)`; `P = V × I × PF`
- AC three-phase (V = line-to-line): `I = P / (√3 × V × PF)`; `P = √3 × V × I × PF`
- The voltage factor k (1 for DC and single-phase, √3 for three-phase) is returned as `k` so the page prints the factor it used (`P = k × V × I × PF`, `I = P / (k × V × PF)`). DC has no standard voltage: a blank voltage in DC mode is an error (`volts_required_dc`), never a silent 230 V. Blank AC voltage = V_1ph or V_3ph.
- Amps to kW page (A6) has a direction switch: `amps_to_kw` (default, `kW = k × V × I × PF / 1000`) or `kw_to_amps` (`I = kW × 1000 / (k × V × PF)`); both return kW, amps and apparent power `VA = k × V × I`.
- kW = W / 1000. `kW = kVA × PF`; `W = VA × PF` (UPS VA ratings: line-interactive often PF 0.6, online 0.8–1.0; read the label)
- `Wh = Ah × V`; `Ah = Wh / V`; usable Wh = Wh × DoD
- 1 unit = 1 kWh. `units = W × hours / 1000`
- Units from amps (E6 "Amps" mode, owner-approved 13 Oct 2026, same page, no separate URL): `units = V × A × PF × hours / 1000` (DC: PF = 1; AC 1-phase default V 230 / 120 US, PF editable; 3-phase adds √3). From amp-hours: `units = Ah × V / 1000`. Keywords "amps to kwh", "amps to units", "amp hours to units" are supporting keywords of E6.
- 1 HP = 745.7 W (motor input watts ≈ HP × 745.7 / motor efficiency; default motor eff 0.8)

## B. Battery / UPS
- Battery-side draw (full audit 2026-10-15 WL-120): `P_bat = P_load / η_inv + P_idle`. `P_idle` = the inverter's own no-load draw, input `idle_w` (W, ≥ 0), default data/appliances.json `ups-idle` running_w (a home UPS/inverter; the page field shows it under Advanced). A DC load (η_inv = 1, no inverter) has `P_idle = 0` whatever is entered. Applies to B1 to B5 (`battery_runtime`, `battery_bank`, `ups_backup`); results return `idle_w`, `battery_w` (= P_bat) and `stored_wh = Ah × V_bank × DoD`. One idle figure for every UPS size [ENGINEER: idle draw grows with inverter size; a per-size default needs datasheets]. The solar battery (D, `solar_battery`), the planner and N7 do not add it yet.
- Runtime (AC load through inverter): `t_h = (Ah × V_bank × DoD) / P_bat` (with P_idle = 0 this is the old `Ah × V × DoD × η_inv / P_load`)
- Runtime (DC load): same with η_inv = 1 (so P_bat = P_load)
- Series strings: V adds, Ah same. Parallel: Ah adds, V same.
- Required bank: `Ah = (P_bat × t_h) / (V_bank × DoD)` (P_bat as above; also returns `battery_kwh_draw = P_bat × t_h / 1000`); batteries = ceil(Ah / Ah_per_battery) × (V_bank / V_battery)
- Loadshedding recharge check: `t_recharge = (Ah × DoD) / (I_charge × η_chg)`; OK if `t_recharge ≤ grid hours between outages`
- UPS capacity: `P_max = VA × PF`; reject if P_load > P_max or surge > inverter surge rating
- Peukert (optional, lead-acid at high current): off by default; if enabled use k = 1.1–1.3 and show the note. Do not apply to LiFePO4.
- Battery current and C-rate (B1 to B5, audit 2026-10-21 WL-177) [ENGINEER]: `I_bat = P_bat / V_bank` (`battery_a`); at the low-battery cut-off `I_cut = P_bat / (V_bank × dc_cutoff_ratio)` (`cutoff_a`, data/defaults.json, the current the cables and fuse must carry); `C = I_bat / Ah` (B4: Ah of the strings bought, else the Ah needed); `high_c_rate` (notice: the lead-acid runtime is optimistic, a lithium BMS may cut off) when `C > max_c_rate_lead_acid` (tubular, lead-acid, AGM, gel) or `> max_c_rate_lifepo4`. The runtime itself is not corrected (no Peukert, as above).
- Guard: P_load ≤ 0 → error, never Infinity/NaN.

## C. Inverter & generator sizing
- Running total: `P_run = Σ P_i` (only appliances on at the same time)
- Surge: `P_surge = P_run + max_i(P_start_i − P_run_i)` (only the largest motor starts at once)
- Inverter: continuous ≥ `P_run × margin`; surge rating ≥ `P_surge`; `kVA = P_run × margin / PF / 1000`
- Generator: `kVA = max(P_run × margin, P_surge) / PF / 1000`, PF 0.8 (generator nameplate convention); the result also carries `running_margin_w = P_run × margin` so pages can show which of the two sets the size. Above the largest `standard_generator_kva` the result carries `above_standard_sizes: true` and the notice is the generator one (`generator_above_standard_sizes`: two generators on separate circuits or a larger industrial set, licensed electrician), not the split-the-circuit text.
- A motor nameplate's locked-rotor amps × voltage is starting **VA** (apparent power at a low start power factor), not watts. To size a generator for it, enter `LRA × V × PF_gen` as the row's starting watts, so `kVA = W / PF_gen / 1000` covers the starting VA.
- Water motor 1 hp (data/appliances.json `water-motor-1hp`) = the C5 rule, `hp × w_per_hp ÷ motor_eff` running and × `start_factors.motor` at start, so one motor has one figure (WL-178). The starting current printed is the starting watts ÷ (V × PF), the surge a UPS or generator must supply; the motor's locked-rotor current in the first instant is higher (pages say so, nameplate value) [ENGINEER].
- Starting factors (defaults, user-editable, engineer to confirm): non-inverter AC/fridge/motor compressor 3×, fridge 4× of small running, inverter AC 1× (soft start), resistive 1×.

## D. Solar
- Daily energy: `E = monthly_units / days_per_month` (or appliance sum). D7 (`solar_size`): a filled `daily_kwh` is used as E; when `monthly_units` is filled too the result carries `usage_both` and the page says the daily figure was used (WL-222). The D7 page pre-fills the monthly units and leaves the daily box blank.
- Array size: `kWp = E / yield_city` (preferred) or `E / (PSH × PR)`
- Panels: `ceil(kWp × 1000 / panel_W)`; roof area = panels × the area of one panel of that size (data/defaults.json `panel_area_m2_by_w`, datasheet module areas; a size between rows takes the area per watt of the row at or below it, so a less efficient class is never shown smaller; audit 2026-10-21 WL-232 [ENGINEER])
- Solar battery: `Ah = (E_night × days) / (V_bank × DoD × η_inv)`
- Panel for 12V battery: `W = (Ah × V × DoD / η_chg) / (PSH × PR)` (PR 0.78: panels rarely deliver STC rating; covers heat, dust, wiring, PWM loss) → round up to standard panel
- Charge controller (MPPT): `A = P_array / V_battery × 1.25`; PWM: `A = Isc × N_parallel × 1.25`
- On-grid sizing cap: net billing size ≤ sanctioned load (NEPRA Prosumer Regulations 2026, secondary sources)

## E. Load, AC, units
- AC units: `units_day = avg_kW × hours`; inverter AC avg_kW default 1.0 for 1.5 ton (range 0.5–1.4, user slider); rated max from spec sheet (Haier 1.5 ton: 410–1,950 W input). Non-inverter 1.5 ton default 1.8 kW while compressor runs × duty 0.8.
- Sanctioned load check: total kW vs sanctioned kW; amps = W / (230 × PF)

## F. Bill (Pakistan A-1 domestic) — data from data/tariff-pk-2026.json, never hard-coded
- Protected (≤200 units every one of last 6 months, non-ToU, <5 kW): slab-wise, one-previous-slab benefit.
- Unprotected: all units × rate of band reached.
- Fixed charge: `Rs/kW × sanctioned kW` from the reached band.
- Add-ons (secondary, monthly re-check): FC surcharge Rs3.23/unit, QTA (quarterly), FPA (monthly input), GST 18%. Show each line separately with source and date.
- Rounding (audit 2026-10-15, WL-109): a bill prints whole rupees, so `bill_total` rounds each line (energy, fixed, FC, QTA, FPA) to the nearest rupee, `subtotal = max(0, Σ rounded lines)`, `GST = round(subtotal × GST% ÷ 100)`, `total = subtotal + GST`; the printed lines always add up to the printed total. `bill_energy` and the N1/net-billing energy lines stay unrounded (they are not a printed bill).
- FPA input range (WL-118): `fpa_per_unit` must lie within data/tariff-pk-2026.json `addons_secondary.fpa_per_unit.min`/`max` (a sanity bound wider than any notified monthly FCA), else `fpa_per_unit_too_small` / `fpa_per_unit_too_large`.
- Average rate (WL-114): `avg_rate = energy (unrounded) ÷ units` (the slab rate at 0 units); the F page tables print it, because a protected bill is charged slab by slab and its average is below the top protected slab's rate.
- Top-slab cliff (WL-117): `cliff_units` = the last closed unprotected band limit; `cliff_rs = bill_total(cliff_units + 1) − bill_total(cliff_units)`, unprotected, same sanctioned load and add-ons as the bill; F1 and the DISCO pages print it in a key fact.
- FPA exemption (audit 2026-10-21, WL-157): the FPA line is 0 only for a category in the FPA exempt list: input `fpa_exempt` (a data/bill-index.json month's list) or else data/tariff-pk-2026.json `addons_secondary.fpa_per_unit.exempt` (lifeline among the modelled categories; NEPRA's July 2026 FCA order para 21(a) spares lifeline, EV charging stations and prepaid). Protected and unprotected homes pay `FPA × fpa_units` (FPA units below); the March 2026 review (SRO 852(I)/2026) spared protected consumers for that month only. `fpa_exempt` (output) = whether this bill's category is in the list.
- FPA units (audit 2026-10-21 Phase 8, WL-244): NEPRA's FCA is billed on the units of the month it adjusts (the reference month, usually two months before the bill), not the units of the bill month. Optional input `fpa_units` (≥ 0, else `fpa_units_too_small`); blank = this bill's units. `fpa_rs = round(FPA × fpa_units)` (0 for an exempt category); output `fpa_units`. The same applies to the ToU bill. Every other line still uses this month's units. Checked against a real LESCO protected bill (September 2026): FPA on July's units, GST on the subtotal including the FPA.
- Energy sum as printed (WL-164): `energy_calc` = the "units × rate" parts the energy charge is made of: protected, one part per protected slab used (`100 × Rs 10.54 + 50 × Rs 13.01`); unprotected and lifeline, one part (units × the band's rate); ToU, peak part + off-peak part. Page "how" text prints it instead of units × the top rate.
- Next slab cliff (WL-165): `next_units` = the limit of the band the bill reached and `next_rs = bill_total(next_units + 1) − bill_total(next_units)` (same sanctioned load and add-ons). Unprotected: the next band's rate on every unit. Protected: `next_units` = the limit of the protected slab reached; one unit past the first slab is still protected (next slab's rate and fixed charge), one unit past the protected limit is an unprotected bill. Not given for lifeline or the open-ended top band (the result cards are then hidden).
- Default add-ons on F1–F12 (WL-161, WL-229): the FPA field is pre-filled with the newest NEPRA-verified month (build `data.fpa_latest`); the FC surcharge and GST fields are blank (placeholders naming the tariff-file values), so a blank uses the category's own default from the tariff file (`by_category`, else `value`; see Bill total).
- ToU bill (WL-162): `bill_tou` = ToU energy + ToU fixed charge (section above) + the same add-on lines as the flat bill (FC surcharge, QTA for the period, FPA, each × units and rounded to whole rupees, category `tou`, not FPA-exempt), then GST on the subtotal: `total_rs = subtotal_rs + gst_rs`. Since audit 2026-10-21 Phase 9b the flat bill pages no longer stop at 5 kW: `bill_total` takes the ToU path (below); `tou_required_at_5kw` is left only in `bill_energy`, which no page reaches at 5 kW.
- Protected check: each history month is read like any numeric input ("1,500" = 1500) and the check runs on the parsed numbers (WL-113).
- Income tax (Income Tax Ordinance 2001 section 235, domestic consumers not on the Active Taxpayers List, bills at or above the threshold in data/tariff-pk-2026.json `income_tax_235`): NOT added by the engine because it depends on the consumer's tax status; every bill page states it in a key fact and an FAQ with the data figures (audit 2026-10-14, WL-14c, WL-49).
- Open tariff questions for the owner's NEPRA/PEC check (audit 2026-10-14, WL-14a/b): (a) unprotected 101–200 units: the engine charges all units at the band reached (non-telescopic); a telescopic reading of SRO 279(I)/2026 would charge 150 units less; (b) fixed charge: the engine charges Rs/kW × sanctioned kW; confirm it is per kW (not per consumer) for every band. Both stay as they are until the SRO text is checked; the pages say the result is an estimate.
- Net billing: `import × slab tariff − export × buyback`; buyback default = the national average energy purchase price (NAEPP) that NEPRA's Prosumer Regulations 2026 (SRO 251(I)/2026, regulation 14(1)(b)) use for exported units; the CY 2026 figure is from NEPRA's power purchase price decision (value, source and links in data/tariff-pk-2026.json `net_billing`). Editable, because NEPRA sets NAEPP for each year and may revise it by notification. Agreements signed before 9 Feb 2026 keep the old net-metering terms until expiry (SRO 547(I)/2026); D10/D11 show old net metering only as the comparison.

## W. Wiring (advisory, always with "confirm with a licensed electrician")
- Design current: `I_b = P / (k × V × PF × η)` (k = √3 three-phase, else 1; DC PF = 1; η = 1 on AC). On DC the watts are what an inverter on the battery delivers, so η = inverter efficiency, input `inverter_eff`, default `inverter_eff_hybrid`, 1 for a DC load wired straight to the battery (full audit 2026-10-15 WL-121) ; from audit 2026-10-21 (WL-177) DC watts use the battery voltage at its low-battery cut-off, `V_cut = V × dc_cutoff_ratio` (data/defaults.json, returned as `current_v`; = V on AC and for entered amps), where the current is highest, in W1/W2/W3 and in W5's watts mode [ENGINEER]. DC device list = `standard_breaker_a` then `standard_dc_fuse_a` (IEC 60269-1 ratings 80 to 400 A, a DC fuse or DC breaker; 300 and 400 A added 2026-10-21) [ENGINEER]. A DC feed above the largest fuse or cable is `dc_load_above_cable_table` (a higher battery voltage or an electrician's busbar design; never "split into circuits", WL-170). W2 has no circuit field: a lighting load type is a lighting circuit (its minimum size and 3% limit), as on W3; an entered `circuit` still wins (WL-125). `cable_rating` is derated like every rating (`ambient_c`, `circuits`; returns `ampacity_30c_b1_a`/`ampacity_30c_c_a` too, WL-126). `voltage_drop` with no watts input at all (W4, amps only) asks for the current (`amps_required`, WL-124); breaker `I_n` = next standard size ≥ I_b; cable ampacity `I_z ≥ max(I_b × 1.25, I_n)` so `I_b ≤ I_n ≤ I_z` always holds; margin < 1 rejected. DC needs the voltage entered on every wiring calculator (`volts_required_dc`), never a silent 230 V (audit 2026-10-14, WL-01).
- Cable rating used everywhere (wire size, MCB size, voltage-drop current check, E4): `I_z = I_z,30 × f_ambient × f_group` (audit 2026-10-14, WL-07). `I_z,30` from data/cable-ampacity.json at 30 °C: two loaded conductors (IEC 60364-5-52 Table B.52.4) for single-phase and DC, three loaded conductors (Table B.52.5, `ampacity_3ph_a`) for three-phase (WL-08). `f_ambient` from Table B.52.14 (PVC) for the air temperature around the cable, input `ambient_c` 10–60 °C (else `ambient_out_of_range`), default data/cable-ampacity.json `derating.default_ambient_c` = 45 °C, a conservative Pakistani-summer design value [ENGINEER, pending PEC review]; `f_group` from Table B.52.17 row 1 for `circuits` sharing a conduit or bundle, 1–20 (else `circuits_out_of_range`), default 1. A value between two table rows takes the factor of the next row up (hotter, more circuits), never interpolated. Results return `ambient_c`, `circuits`, `ambient_factor`, `group_factor`, `derate`, `ampacity_30c_a` (table value) and `ampacity_a` (derated, the one compared).
- Voltage drop single-phase and DC: `ΔV = 2 × L × I × ρ / A`; three-phase: `ΔV = √3 × L × I × ρ / A` (drop factor returned as `kv`); with watts entered `I = P / (k × V × PF × η)` (η = inverter efficiency on DC as in the design current above, input `inverter_eff`, 1 on AC; full audit WL-121, so W5 = W2), k = √3 for three-phase (returned as `k`); target ≤ 3% (lighting) / 5% (other). Conductor select: ρ_copper = 0.0225, ρ_aluminium = 0.036 Ω·mm²/m, both IEC 60364-5-52 Annex G ρ1 = 1.25 × ρ20, which states these two values for conductors in normal service (aluminium value [ENGINEER]); an entered ρ overrides both. DC mode needs the voltage entered (`volts_required_dc`).
- Voltage drop current check (copper only): rated size = largest cable in data/cable-ampacity.json with area ≤ the entered area (a PK n/d area such as 2.98 mm² is rated as 2.5); `over_cable_rating = I > I_z(method, default B1, derated as above; three-phase column in ac3)`, or true when the area is below the smallest table cable. The page shows a warning whenever it is true, whatever the voltage drop. Aluminium: `ampacity_checked = false` and the page says the current rating is not checked (no aluminium ampacity data yet).
- PK cable designation n/d (n strands, strand diameter d in thou = 0.001 inch): `A = n × π × (d_mm / 2)²` with `d_mm = d_thou × 0.0254 = d_inch × 25.4` → 3/29 ≈ 1.28 mm², 7/29 ≈ 2.98 mm², 7/36 ≈ 4.6, 7/44 ≈ 6.9, 7/52 ≈ 9.6, 7/64 ≈ 14.5, 19/44 ≈ 18.6, 19/52 ≈ 26, 19/64 ≈ 39.4. Engine input `strand_in` is in inches (0.029 for /29). One rule for every page (audit 2026-10-14, WL-09): a PK size is rated as the largest IEC size not above its real area (data/cable-ampacity.json `rated_as_mm2`: 7/52 → 6, 7/64 → 10, 19/44 → 16), which is what the voltage-drop check does with the real area; and the PK size printed for an IEC result is the smallest one whose real area is at least that IEC size, so its real voltage drop is never above the one computed.
- Ampacity table: IEC 60364-5-52 values for PVC copper, method B1/C, 1 to 120 mm² (50 to 120 mm² added 2026-10-21 for battery cables, WL-170) (engineer to check against PK maker datasheets). Large n/d sizes 19/72, 19/83, 37/72, 37/83 added with the same rating rule. Shop name (WL-180): where the metric size a shop sells an n/d cable as (`sold_as_mm2`) differs from the size shown, the n/d name is printed as "7/64, sold as 16 mm²" [ENGINEER: confirm the shop names].
- Solar PV cable (W4, `voltage_drop` with `cable: pv`, WL-180) [ENGINEER]: `I_z = I_z,EN50618(method) × f_ambient`: data/cable-ampacity.json `pv_cables` (EN 50618 Table A.3 at 60 °C air: on a surface (default), in free air, two cables touching) × Table A.4 factor for the roof air (`ambient_c`, default `pv_cables.default_ambient_c`; below 60 °C the factor stays 1); `over_cable_rating` when `1.25 × I` (`isc_factor`, IEC 62548-1: the cable carries 1.25 × the string Isc; the page asks for Isc) is above `I_z`. House-wiring PVC ratings are not used for PV cable.

## G. New tools (added 13 Oct 2026). Assumptions marked [ENGINEER] need engineer sign-off before launch.
- **Bill audit (N1):** `days = reading_date − previous_reading_date`; `units_30 = units × bill_month_days / days` (bill_month_days = 30, data/defaults.json). When protection is at risk (below), the fair bill is the protected bill the home should have had: protected slab-wise energy with every protected slab limit × `days ÷ bill_month_days`, plus the fixed charge of the protected band reached, `fair_protected = true` (audit 2026-10-15, WL-111; the engine assumes the home was protected before, which the page says). Flags: days > 31 (billing-cycle stretch can push slab: recompute bill at units_30 and show the difference); units − previous-year-same-month > 50% (spike); protection at risk (billed units over the protected limit while the 30-day equivalent is within it, so the stretch alone cost the status). Output is an estimate, never an official finding. Decided 2026-10-14 (audit WL-21): three checks stay page guidance, not calculations, because the engine has no verified input for them: the six-month protected history (the page explains the rule and the flag above covers the cycle case), the FPA/QTA line check (the notified FPA per DISCO and month is not verified in data; the page tells the user to compare each line with the notified rate × units), and detection units (the page explains the right to the detection proforma). Implement them only with a verified per-month FPA source and a history input.
- **Sanctioned load (N4):** `connected_kW = Σ rated_W / 1000`; `demand_kW = connected_kW × diversity` (default 0.6 home, [ENGINEER]); recommend `max(1, ceil(demand_kW))` kW (one rule, `sanctionedFor` in engine/sanction.js, shared with E4, WL-110) (a motor's starting surge lasts seconds and does not register on the MDI, so it is shown only as a note for inverter/generator sizing, [ENGINEER]); warn at ≥ 5 kW (ToU meter mandatory, not protected) and show net-billing cap = sanctioned kW.
- **Winter heating (N5):** heat needed Q (kW) user input or from room size; AC heat mode `cost/h = Q / COP × tariff` (COP default 3.0 at ~7 °C outdoor, user editable, [ENGINEER]); electric heater `cost/h = Q / 1.0 × tariff`; gas heater `cost/h = Q / η_gas × 3.6 / CV_gas × gas_price` (η 0.8 for flued, CV ≈ 37 MJ/m³ — confirm against OGRA/SNGPL data; gas price input in Rs/m³, or in Rs per MMBtu as printed on the gas bill: `Rs/m³ = Rs/MMBtu × CV / 1055.056` (MJ per MMBtu, data/defaults.json `mj_per_mmbtu`); the N5 page default is a domestic slab price from data/tariff-pk-2026.json `gas_domestic`, secondary source, not yet verified against an OGRA notification).
- **Solar quote checker (N3):** `Rs_per_W = total_price / (kWp × 1000)`; compare with data/solar-prices.json ranges (dated); checklist of included items (structure, DC/AC cables, breakers/SPD, earthing, net-billing fees, warranty years); verdict = below / within / above range. Never names an installer as cheating.
- **Solar forecast (N6):** `units_day = kWp × Σ_hours(GTI_forecast_kW/m²) × PR` using a free forecast API (Open-Meteo GTI or radiation × transposition); PR 0.78; show as estimate with cloud cover.
- **UPS vs solar vs generator 5-year cost (N7):** `TCO = capex + Σ_years(energy_cost + maintenance) + replacements` (lead-acid every 2 yrs, LiFePO4 8+ yrs, [ENGINEER]); generator fuel `L/h = fuel_rate(kVA, load%)` from data/generator-fuel.json; grid recharge energy for UPS = backup_kWh / (η_chg × η_inv).
- **E-bike charging (N8):** `kWh_charge = Ah × V / 1000 / η_charger` (η 0.85); `Rs_per_charge = kWh_charge × tariff`; `Rs_per_km = Rs_per_charge / range_km`.
- **AC temperature (N9):** `saving% = per_degree% × (T_new − T_old)`; per_degree default 6%/°C as an editable rule of thumb, labelled as approximate [ENGINEER]; `units_saved = monthly_AC_units × saving%`.

## Engine contract and clarifications (Phase 1, 2026-10-13)
Each calculator is `engine/<file>.js` → `calc(inputs, data)` returning `{ ok: true, ...values }` or
`{ ok: false, error: code, field }`. `data = { defaults, tariff, cities }` from `data/*.json`. The `calc`
id is the one used in `calculator-test-cases.json`. Error codes map to user messages in `engine/messages.js`.
These rules fill gaps in the sections above; they are part of the formulas:
- **Rounding up** (panels, batteries, kW, standard sizes) ignores floating-point dust: 2.9999999 / 3.0000001 → 3.
- **Standard sizes** (panels, charge controllers, breakers) come from `data/defaults.json`; the result shows the next size up.
- **Battery bank count**: `series = V_bank / V_battery` (must be a whole number, else error), `strings = ceil(Ah_required / Ah_battery)`, `batteries = series × strings`.
- **UPS capacity** output key is `can_run` (`ok` is reserved for the result envelope). If one surge field is filled the other is required; both blank skips the surge check.
- **Standard sizes exceeded** (controller > 100 A): result carries `above_standard_sizes: true`; a wiring breaker above the largest standard size is the error `load_above_standard_breakers` (the unused `design_current` calculator was removed 2026-10-14, its cases now run on `breaker_size`) and the UI says to split the circuit/array or consult an engineer; a generator above the largest standard kVA gets its own notice (two generators or an industrial set, section C).
- **Input error codes** (audit 2026-10-14, WL-29): a calculator may give an input its own error code. A code ending in `_invalid` or `_out_of_range` covers every failure of that input; any other own code (a range code such as `load_must_be_positive`, or a required code such as `usage_required`) is used only for a blank or out-of-range value, while text that is not a number gives `<field>_invalid` and a value beyond the input cap gives `<field>_too_large`. Appliance-row codes carry the row from 0 (`load_<row>_<column>_invalid`); messages count rows from 1 and name the column's rule (hours 0 to 24). A choice field's `_invalid` asks the user to choose from the list.
- **Loads list**: `start` defaults to `run`; `start < run` is an error; `qty` multiplies running watts; only the largest single start step is added for surge.
- **Sanctioned load**: recommended = max(min_sanctioned_kw, ceil(demand)); empty appliance list is an error; ToU warning when recommended ≥ `tou_threshold_kw` (5).
- **Bill, lifeline**: only when the user ticks lifeline; ≤ 100 units; all units at the lifeline band reached (1–50 → 3.95, 51–100 → 7.74), no fixed charge, no QTA. [VERIFY against a DISCO lifeline bill]
- **Bill, protected**: > 200 units is an error (protected cannot apply). **Bill, ≥ 5 kW**: must use the ToU calculation (`bill_tou`); `bill_energy` refuses it; `bill_total` (F1–F12, F14, E1, N16), net billing (D10, D11) and the H1 planner take the ToU path (section F, audit 2026-10-21 Phase 9 and 9b). ToU fixed basis = max(`tou.fixed_min_fraction` (0.5) × sanctioned, MDI), from the tariff file.
- **Bill total**: subtotal = energy + fixed + FC surcharge × units + QTA × units + FPA × fpa_units (blank = units, WL-244); GST % applied to the subtotal. QTA/FPA may be negative (shown as credit lines); the subtotal is never below 0. Add-on rates are inputs; a blank field uses `tariff-pk-2026.json` `addons_secondary` (FC surcharge and GST: the bill category's `by_category` value when listed, else `value`; QTA for the chosen quarter `period`; FPA if notified else 0), each shown with its source and date. FC surcharge `by_category`: lifeline 0 (NEPRA FC surcharge decision, 6 March 2023, lifeline row blank; audit 2026-10-21 WL-241); protected 0 (a real LESCO protected bill, September 2026: no FC surcharge line, total reconciles only with 0; audit 2026-10-21 Phase 8 WL-243). GST `by_category`: lifeline 0 until confirmed [VERIFY, WL-241: secondary sources say lifeline pays GST]; protected pays the full value on the whole subtotal, FPA included (same LESCO bill, WL-244). [VERIFY GST base]
- **Net billing**: if export credit > import energy charge, `net_energy_rs = 0` and the excess is `carry_forward_rs` (never a negative bill).
- **Bill audit (N1)**: slab jump = the 30-day-equivalent units fall in a LOWER unprotected band than billed units (a short cycle never flags). Fair estimate = all billed units × rate of the band the 30-day equivalent reaches + that band's fixed charge, or, when protection is at risk, the pro-rated protected bill (WL-111, above); `extra_rs = billed − fair` (min 0); `fair_basis` names which. Long cycle = days > 31. Spike = units > previous-year units × 1.5; N1 shows it as a result card when last year's units are entered (audit 2026-10-21, WL-163).
- **AC temperature (N9)**: saving capped at 100%; lowering the set point returns `extra_pct`/`extra_units`, never a negative saving. Set points limited to 16–30 °C.
- **Cleaning (N12)**: with a cleaning cost, `clean_after_days` = first day n where Σ_{k=1..n} kWp × yield × min(rate × k, cap) × tariff > cleaning cost (searched up to 365 days).
- **TCO (N7)**: `replacements = floor((years − ε) / life_years)`; fuel L/h table comes later from `data/generator-fuel.json`, until then energy cost per year is an input.
- **Autonomy (N14)**: if cloudy-day solar ≥ load, no day count is shown; the message says the solar covers the load.
- **Voltage drop**: if ΔV ≥ supply voltage the cable is rejected with a clear message (no negative voltage at load).
- **Display rounding (audit 2026-10-14, WL-16)**: the engine keeps full precision; only the printed figure is rounded. A minimum size the user must buy (Ah of a battery bank, inverter kW/kVA) is rounded UP (format `up`, `up1`, `up2`), so the page never shows less than the engine needs; a runtime or backup time is printed in hours and minutes with the minutes rounded DOWN (format `hm`), so it is never overstated. Standard sizes picked by the engine (MCB, cable, controller, panel, generator) are already whole steps. Page text that names a user input prints it as entered (no `|int` on an input), and the live "How this was calculated" text prints the values the engine used (`out.*`), never a default the user may have changed (WL-12; `tests/calc-text.test.js`).
- **Inputs**: PF/efficiency/DoD must be 0.05–1, voltages ≥ 1 V; lifeline bills default FC surcharge and GST to 0 from the tariff file's `by_category` (GST unverified; user can enter them); plain decimal text ("1500", "12.5") is accepted, also with thousands commas in groups of three or the Pakistani lakh grouping ("1,500", "12,000.5", "1,50,000"; audit 2026-10-14, WL-17), while any other comma ("1,5", "15,00") is an error, never read as a decimal point (hex/exponent text rejected), any value above 1e12 is rejected; yes/no ticks accept true/'true'/'on'/'yes'/1; blank uses the default; anything non-numeric, negative where not allowed, PF/efficiency/DoD outside (0, 1] is an error, never NaN.
- **Input checks (audit 2026-10-21 Phase 3):** sanity bounds from data/defaults.json: design margin `1 ≤ margin ≤ margin_max` (every page with a margin), peak sun hours `psh_min ≤ PSH ≤ psh_max` (D6, and the PSH path of the yield when no city is picked), battery/UPS load `≤ load_w_max` (B1–B5, `load_w_too_large`). A broken bound's error carries the bound (`limit`) and the message prints it ("must be at least 1", "must be 3 or less"). A box the selected mode does not use is still checked when filled, never silently accepted: E5 average draw with a preset AC, D16 Isc and parallel strings with MPPT and panel watts with PWM, E6 amps, voltage and PF in watts mode, W2/W5 inverter efficiency on AC, A6 the other direction's box (WL-223). Messages for generic codes name the field with its unit and, where the page has one, its default as an example (WL-226); the list-only "hours for at least one appliance" message has its own code `loads_hours_required` (D3/D8), so a plain hours box (B4, D15) gets its own field message.
- **Data in the engine**: `data = { defaults, tariff, cities, appliances, cables }`; `appliances` is data/appliances.json keyed by id, `cables` is data/cable-ampacity.json (loaded 2026-10-13 from IEC 60364-5-52 Table B.52.4; three-phase column B.52.5 and correction factors B.52.14 / B.52.17 added 2026-10-14; engineer to confirm).
