# Wattlyst calculator formulas (single source of truth)

Status: drafted 2026-10-13, implemented in `engine/` 2026-10-13 (Phase 1). Every formula here must pass `calculator-test-cases.json` before a page ships.
All defaults below live in `data/defaults.json` (the engine reads them from there; this table documents them).
Engineer review required before launch (see engineer-reviewer.md). Units are SI unless stated.

## Global defaults (editable by user, shown on the page)
| Symbol | Meaning | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| V_1ph | Single-phase voltage | 230 V (PK), 120 V (US toggle) | NEPRA Performance Standards: 400/230 V, 50 Hz, ±5% |
| V_3ph | Three-phase line-to-line | 400 V | same |
| PF | Power factor (AC loads) | 0.8 for mixed/motor loads, 1.0 resistive (heater, iron, geyser) | industry convention |
| η_inv | Inverter/UPS efficiency | 0.85 (UPS/older), 0.90–0.93 (modern hybrid) | typical datasheets |
| DoD | Usable depth of discharge | lead-acid/tubular 0.5, LiFePO4 0.9 | battery life convention |
| η_chg | Charging efficiency | lead-acid 0.85, LiFePO4 0.95 | typical |
| margin | Sizing safety margin | 1.25 | continuous-load practice |
| yield | PV output kWh per kWp per day | from data/cities-pk.json (Global Solar Atlas) | Lahore 4.0, Karachi 4.6 ... |
| PR | Performance ratio (if PSH used instead of yield) | 0.78 | matches GSA yield/PSH |

## A. Conversions
- DC: `I = P / V`; `P = V × I`
- AC single-phase: `I = P / (V × PF)`; `P = V × I × PF`
- AC three-phase (V = line-to-line): `I = P / (√3 × V × PF)`; `P = √3 × V × I × PF`
- The voltage factor k (1 for DC and single-phase, √3 for three-phase) is returned as `k` so the page prints the factor it used (`P = k × V × I × PF`, `I = P / (k × V × PF)`). DC has no standard voltage: a blank voltage in DC mode is an error (`volts_required_dc`), never a silent 230 V. Blank AC voltage = V_1ph or V_3ph.
- Amps to kW page (A6) has a direction switch: `amps_to_kw` (default, `kW = k × V × I × PF / 1000`) or `kw_to_amps` (`I = kW × 1000 / (k × V × PF)`); both return kW, amps and apparent power `VA = k × V × I`.
- kW = W / 1000. `kW = kVA × PF`; `W = VA × PF` (UPS VA ratings: line-interactive often PF 0.6, online 0.8–1.0; read the label)
- `Wh = Ah × V`; `Ah = Wh / V`; usable Wh = Wh × DoD
- 1 unit = 1 kWh. `units = W × hours / 1000`
- Units from amps (E6 "Amps" mode, owner-approved 13 Oct 2026, same page, no separate URL): `units = V × A × PF × hours / 1000` (DC: PF = 1; AC 1-phase default V 230 / 120 US, PF editable; 3-phase adds √3). From amp-hours: `units = Ah × V / 1000`. Keywords "amps to kwh", "amps to units", "amp hours to units" are supporting keywords of E6.
- 1 HP = 745.7 W (motor input watts ≈ HP × 745.7 / motor efficiency; default motor eff 0.8)

## B. Battery / UPS
- Runtime (AC load through inverter): `t_h = (Ah × V_bank × DoD × η_inv) / P_load`
- Runtime (DC load): same with η_inv = 1
- Series strings: V adds, Ah same. Parallel: Ah adds, V same.
- Required bank: `Ah = (P_load × t_h) / (V_bank × DoD × η_inv)`; batteries = ceil(Ah / Ah_per_battery) × (V_bank / V_battery)
- Loadshedding recharge check: `t_recharge = (Ah × DoD) / (I_charge × η_chg)`; OK if `t_recharge ≤ grid hours between outages`
- UPS capacity: `P_max = VA × PF`; reject if P_load > P_max or surge > inverter surge rating
- Peukert (optional, lead-acid at high current): off by default; if enabled use k = 1.1–1.3 and show the note. Do not apply to LiFePO4.
- Guard: P_load ≤ 0 → error, never Infinity/NaN.

## C. Inverter & generator sizing
- Running total: `P_run = Σ P_i` (only appliances on at the same time)
- Surge: `P_surge = P_run + max_i(P_start_i − P_run_i)` (only the largest motor starts at once)
- Inverter: continuous ≥ `P_run × margin`; surge rating ≥ `P_surge`; `kVA = P_run × margin / PF / 1000`
- Generator: `kVA = max(P_run × margin, P_surge) / PF / 1000`, PF 0.8 (generator nameplate convention); the result also carries `running_margin_w = P_run × margin` so pages can show which of the two sets the size. Above the largest `standard_generator_kva` the result carries `above_standard_sizes: true` and the notice is the generator one (`generator_above_standard_sizes`: two generators on separate circuits or a larger industrial set, licensed electrician), not the split-the-circuit text.
- A motor nameplate's locked-rotor amps × voltage is starting **VA** (apparent power at a low start power factor), not watts. To size a generator for it, enter `LRA × V × PF_gen` as the row's starting watts, so `kVA = W / PF_gen / 1000` covers the starting VA.
- Starting factors (defaults, user-editable, engineer to confirm): non-inverter AC/fridge/motor compressor 3×, fridge 4× of small running, inverter AC 1× (soft start), resistive 1×.

## D. Solar
- Daily energy: `E = monthly_units / 30` (or appliance sum)
- Array size: `kWp = E / yield_city` (preferred) or `E / (PSH × PR)`
- Panels: `ceil(kWp × 1000 / panel_W)`; roof area ≈ panels × 2.6 m² (585 W class, ~2.28 × 1.13 m) — confirm per datasheet
- Solar battery: `Ah = (E_night × days) / (V_bank × DoD × η_inv)`
- Panel for 12V battery: `W = (Ah × V × DoD / η_chg) / (PSH × PR)` (PR 0.78: panels rarely deliver STC rating; covers heat, dust, wiring, PWM loss) → round up to standard panel
- Charge controller (MPPT): `A = P_array / V_battery × 1.25`; PWM: `A = Isc × N_parallel × 1.25`
- On-grid sizing cap: net billing size ≤ sanctioned load (NEPRA Prosumer Regulations 2026, secondary sources)

## E. Load, AC, units
- AC units: `units_day = avg_kW × hours`; inverter AC avg_kW default 1.0 for 1.5 ton (range 0.5–1.4, user slider); rated max from spec sheet (Haier 1.5 ton: 410–1,950 W input). Non-inverter 1.5 ton default 1.8 kW while compressor runs × duty 0.8.
- Sanctioned load check: total kW vs sanctioned kW; amps = W / (230 × PF)

## F. Bill (Pakistan A-1 domestic) — data from data/tariff-pk-2026.json, never hard-coded
- Protected (≤200 units every one of last 6 months, non-ToU, <5 kW): slab-wise, one-previous-slab benefit.
- Unprotected: all units × rate of band reached.
- Fixed charge: `Rs/kW × sanctioned kW` from the reached band.
- Add-ons (secondary, monthly re-check): FC surcharge Rs3.23/unit, QTA (quarterly), FPA (monthly input), GST 18%. Show each line separately with source and date.
- Net billing: `import × slab tariff − export × buyback`; buyback default 11 (editable, label "not yet confirmed on a NEPRA page").

## W. Wiring (advisory, always with "confirm with a licensed electrician")
- Design current: `I_b = P / (k × V × PF)` (k = √3 three-phase, else 1; DC PF = 1); breaker `I_n` = next standard size ≥ I_b; cable ampacity `I_z ≥ max(I_b × 1.25, I_n)` so `I_b ≤ I_n ≤ I_z` always holds; margin < 1 rejected. DC needs the voltage entered on every wiring calculator (`volts_required_dc`), never a silent 230 V (audit 2026-10-14, WL-01).
- Cable rating used everywhere (wire size, MCB size, voltage-drop current check, E4): `I_z = I_z,30 × f_ambient × f_group` (audit 2026-10-14, WL-07). `I_z,30` from data/cable-ampacity.json at 30 °C: two loaded conductors (IEC 60364-5-52 Table B.52.4) for single-phase and DC, three loaded conductors (Table B.52.5, `ampacity_3ph_a`) for three-phase (WL-08). `f_ambient` from Table B.52.14 (PVC) for the air temperature around the cable, input `ambient_c` 10–60 °C (else `ambient_out_of_range`), default data/cable-ampacity.json `derating.default_ambient_c` = 45 °C, a conservative Pakistani-summer design value [ENGINEER, pending PEC review]; `f_group` from Table B.52.17 row 1 for `circuits` sharing a conduit or bundle, 1–20 (else `circuits_out_of_range`), default 1. A value between two table rows takes the factor of the next row up (hotter, more circuits), never interpolated. Results return `ambient_c`, `circuits`, `ambient_factor`, `group_factor`, `derate`, `ampacity_30c_a` (table value) and `ampacity_a` (derated, the one compared).
- Voltage drop single-phase and DC: `ΔV = 2 × L × I × ρ / A`; three-phase: `ΔV = √3 × L × I × ρ / A` (drop factor returned as `kv`); with watts entered `I = P / (k × V × PF)`, k = √3 for three-phase (returned as `k`); target ≤ 3% (lighting) / 5% (other). Conductor select: ρ_copper = 0.0225, ρ_aluminium = 0.036 Ω·mm²/m, both IEC 60364-5-52 Annex G ρ1 = 1.25 × ρ20, which states these two values for conductors in normal service (aluminium value [ENGINEER]); an entered ρ overrides both. DC mode needs the voltage entered (`volts_required_dc`).
- Voltage drop current check (copper only): rated size = largest cable in data/cable-ampacity.json with area ≤ the entered area (a PK n/d area such as 2.98 mm² is rated as 2.5); `over_cable_rating = I > I_z(method, default B1, derated as above; three-phase column in ac3)`, or true when the area is below the smallest table cable. The page shows a warning whenever it is true, whatever the voltage drop. Aluminium: `ampacity_checked = false` and the page says the current rating is not checked (no aluminium ampacity data yet).
- PK cable designation n/d (n strands, strand diameter d in thou = 0.001 inch): `A = n × π × (d_mm / 2)²` with `d_mm = d_thou × 0.0254 = d_inch × 25.4` → 3/29 ≈ 1.28 mm², 7/29 ≈ 2.98 mm², 7/36 ≈ 4.6, 7/44 ≈ 6.9, 7/52 ≈ 9.6, 7/64 ≈ 14.5, 19/44 ≈ 18.6, 19/52 ≈ 26, 19/64 ≈ 39.4. Engine input `strand_in` is in inches (0.029 for /29). One rule for every page (audit 2026-10-14, WL-09): a PK size is rated as the largest IEC size not above its real area (data/cable-ampacity.json `rated_as_mm2`: 7/52 → 6, 7/64 → 10, 19/44 → 16), which is what the voltage-drop check does with the real area; and the PK size printed for an IEC result is the smallest one whose real area is at least that IEC size, so its real voltage drop is never above the one computed.
- Ampacity table: IEC 60364-5-52 values for PVC copper, method B1/C (engineer to load exact table + PK maker datasheets into data/cable-ampacity.json)

## G. New tools (added 13 Oct 2026). Assumptions marked [ENGINEER] need engineer sign-off before launch.
- **Bill audit (N1):** `days = reading_date − previous_reading_date`; `units_30 = units × 30 / days`. Flags: days > 31 (billing-cycle stretch can push slab: recompute bill at units_30 and show the difference); units − previous-year-same-month > 50% (spike); protected status lost although all 6 months ≤ 200 (cite history); FPA/QTA line ≠ notified rate × units; "detection units" line present → explain the user's right to ask for the detection proforma. Output is an estimate, never an official finding.
- **Sanctioned load (N4):** `connected_kW = Σ rated_W / 1000`; `demand_kW = connected_kW × diversity` (default 0.6 home, [ENGINEER]); recommend `max(1, ceil(demand_kW))` kW (a motor's starting surge lasts seconds and does not register on the MDI, so it is shown only as a note for inverter/generator sizing, [ENGINEER]); warn at ≥ 5 kW (ToU meter mandatory, not protected) and show net-billing cap = sanctioned kW.
- **Winter heating (N5):** heat needed Q (kW) user input or from room size; AC heat mode `cost/h = Q / COP × tariff` (COP default 3.0 at ~7 °C outdoor, user editable, [ENGINEER]); electric heater `cost/h = Q / 1.0 × tariff`; gas heater `cost/h = Q / η_gas × 3.6 / CV_gas × gas_price` (η 0.8 for flued, CV ≈ 37 MJ/m³ — confirm against OGRA/SNGPL data; gas price input in Rs/m³, or in Rs per MMBtu as printed on the gas bill: `Rs/m³ = Rs/MMBtu × CV / 1055.056` (MJ per MMBtu, data/defaults.json `mj_per_mmbtu`); the N5 page default is a domestic slab price from data/tariff-pk-2026.json `gas_domestic`, secondary source, not yet verified against an OGRA notification).
- **Solar quote checker (N3):** `Rs_per_W = total_price / (kWp × 1000)`; compare with data/solar-prices.json ranges (dated); checklist of included items (structure, DC/AC cables, breakers/SPD, earthing, net-billing fees, warranty years); verdict = below / within / above range. Never names an installer as cheating.
- **Solar forecast (N6):** `units_day = kWp × Σ_hours(GTI_forecast_kW/m²) × PR` using a free forecast API (Open-Meteo GTI or radiation × transposition); PR 0.78; show as estimate with cloud cover.
- **UPS vs solar vs generator 5-year cost (N7):** `TCO = capex + Σ_years(energy_cost + maintenance) + replacements` (lead-acid every 2 yrs, LiFePO4 8+ yrs, [ENGINEER]); generator fuel `L/h = fuel_rate(kVA, load%)` from data/generator-fuel.json; grid recharge energy for UPS = backup_kWh / (η_chg × η_inv).
- **E-bike charging (N8):** `kWh_charge = Ah × V / 1000 / η_charger` (η 0.85); `Rs_per_charge = kWh_charge × tariff`; `Rs_per_km = Rs_per_charge / range_km`.
- **AC temperature (N9):** `saving% = per_degree% × (T_new − T_old)`; per_degree default 6%/°C as an editable rule of thumb, labelled as approximate [ENGINEER]; `units_saved = monthly_AC_units × saving%`.
- **Inverter AC payback (N10):** both ACs on their average draw (section E): `kW_noninv_avg = kW_noninv_running × duty` (duty default data/defaults.json `ac_noninverter_duty`; 1 = peak summer, compressor never cycles off), `kW_inv` = inverter AC average; `units_x = kW_x_avg × hours_year` (0 < hours_year ≤ 8,760, else `hours_year_invalid`); `rs_x = units_x × tariff`; `annual_saving = (units_noninv − units_inv) × tariff`; `payback_months = price_difference / (annual_saving / 12)`, in calendar months (the saving is a yearly figure, so idle winter months are already inside it). Error `inverter_ac_must_use_less` when `kW_inv ≥ kW_noninv_avg`.
- **Series/parallel (N11):** `V_total = n_series × V_cell`; `Ah_total = n_parallel × Ah`; `kWh = V_total × Ah_total / 1000`. Only identical batteries; warn on mixing chemistries/ages.
- **Cleaning loss (N12):** `loss_kWh_day = kWp × yield × soiling%`; soiling% grows `rate%/day × days_since_clean` capped at 25% (rate default 0.3%/day for dusty cities, [ENGINEER], research-based; entered as a share, at most data/defaults.json `soiling_rate_max_per_day` 0.05, else `soiling_rate_invalid`, so 0.3 typed for 0.3% is caught); `Rs_lost = loss_kWh × tariff_or_buyback`; clean when Rs_lost_since_clean > cleaning_cost.
- **Mosque/shop solar (N13):** load profile templates (prayer-time AC/fan blocks, Jummah peak; shop 10:00–22:00) → daily kWh → solar sizing (section D).
- **Autonomy (N14):** `days = (Ah × V × DoD × η_inv) / (daily_load_Wh − daily_solar_Wh_cloudy)`; cloudy solar = normal × 0.2–0.4 (user editable). Bank for a chosen number of days (optional `target_days` > 0): `target_Ah = target_days × (daily_load_Wh − daily_solar_Wh_cloudy) / (V × DoD × η_inv)`. `daily_load_Wh` is the energy the house takes from the solar inverter in a day (the panels' cloudy-day Wh are subtracted from it).

## Phase 3 tools (added 2026-10-13, Wave 1 pages). Assumptions marked [ENGINEER] need engineer sign-off.
- **Appliance rows** (E1 home load, D4 inverter, generator, D8 solar from loads): rows `{ id, watts, start, qty, hours }` (plain `{ run, start, qty }` rows are accepted too). `id` from data/appliances.json gives running W, starting W and typical hours/day; `watts` overrides running W (starting W scales in proportion); `id` "custom" or blank needs `watts`; optional `start` (starting W; blank = auto) sets the row's starting watts, so a custom motor carries its surge (a custom row without it starts at its running watts; on a listed appliance it overrides the scaled `starting_w`); `start` < running W is an error. Pages that size for the surge (generator pages C1–C4, C7) show a Starting watts column via the field flag `"start": true`, and the `?load=` share link carries it as a fifth part of each row; qty ≥ 0 (default 1); hours 0–24. Energy per row uses the appliance's average draw `avg_w` where one is known (inverter ACs, section E), scaled with any watts override; amps and peak always use the running/starting watts. The starting step of an appliance row is its own `starting_w` from data/appliances.json (e.g. non-inverter fridge 1,200 W on 200 W running = 6×), not the generic `start_factors` of data/defaults.json, which apply only where the engine builds a load from a factor (C5 pump) or the page quotes a rule of thumb. [ENGINEER: `start_factors.fridge` = 4 and the fridge row's 6× disagree; pick one.]
- **Home load (E1):** `P_run = Σ run × qty`; `P_peak = P_run + max(start − run)` (section C, one motor starts at a time); `I = P / (V × PF)` at running and at peak; `units_day = Σ run × qty × hours / 1000`; `units_month = units_day × days` (30); optional `Rs = units_month × tariff` and sanctioned-load check (section E).
- **Add an AC (E4):** `P_new = P_current + P_ac_run`; `P_peak = P_current + P_ac_start`; amps as above; `within = P_new / 1000 ≤ sanctioned kW`; the AC's own circuit (MCB, curve, cable) uses the MCB size rule (W3) with the AC as the appliance and the AC's own PF (`pf_by_load_type`; the page's PF field is the home's mixed PF and is used only for the home's amps; the AC's own running amps `ac_run_amps` use the AC's PF), with the same derating inputs, so this page and the wiring pages print the same breaker (audit 2026-10-14, WL-10; tests/guardrails.test.js checks every AC).
- **Generator for an AC (C6):** loads = [AC running/starting from data/appliances.json] + optional other loads (run, start ≥ run); section C generator formula; `standard_kva` = next size in data/defaults.json `standard_generator_kva` [ENGINEER].
- **Generator for a pump (C5):** `P_run = HP × 745.7 / η_motor` (section A, η 0.8); `P_start = P_run × k_start` (`start_factors.motor` = 3, [ENGINEER]); then section C.
- **UPS backup (B5):** `P_max = VA × PF`; load > P_max (or surge > surge rating) is an error, never a shorter time; otherwise runtime from section B.
- **Solar for an AC (D5) and solar from loads (D8):** `E_day = avg_W × hours / 1000` (AC: `avg_w` from data/appliances.json, section E; loads: Σ run × qty × hours); `kWp_energy = E_day / yield`; `kWp_power = P_run / 1000 / PR` (the array must carry the running load at midday without a battery); `kWp = max(kWp_energy, kWp_power)`; panels = ceil(kWp × 1000 / panel_W); inverter continuous `= max(P_run × margin, installed_kWp × 1000 / dc_ac_ratio_max)` (2026-10-14: the array may exceed the inverter by at most the usual DC/AC oversize, data/defaults.json `dc_ac_ratio_max` 1.3 [ENGINEER]), surge ≥ `P_start`; optional night battery `Ah = (P_run × night_hours) / (V_bank × DoD × η_inv)` (section D, LiFePO4 DoD 0.9, hybrid η 0.9, V_bank default 24 V).
- **Panel for a 12 V battery (D6) additions:** `fitted_W` = standard panel size chosen; `t_charge = (Ah × V × DoD / η_chg) / (fitted_W × PR)` hours of peak sun, `days = t_charge / PSH`; MPPT controller `A = fitted_W / V × margin` → next standard size.
- **AC units (E5):** `avg_kW` = `avg_w` of the chosen AC in data/appliances.json ÷ 1000 (inverter 1 ton 0.65, 1.5 ton 1.0, 2 ton 1.3 [ENGINEER]; non-inverter = running W × duty 0.8), or a custom kW; `units = avg_kW × hours`; cost lines `= units × tariff` per hour, day and month.
- **Net billing vs old net metering (D10):** old net metering `units_net = max(0, import − export)`, billed at the band that `units_net` reaches (energy only); `extra_vs_net_metering = max(0, net_billing_energy − net_metering_energy)`; excess export under old rules carries forward as units.
- **Wire size (W1, W2):** `I_b` from section W (an appliance id sets P: inverter AC at its maximum rated input `starting_w`, others at running W); breaker `I_n` = next standard size ≥ I_b, but ≥ `I_b × margin` for motor-type loads (motor, compressor, inverter compressor, high inrush) [ENGINEER]; cable `I_z ≥ max(I_b × margin, I_n)` from data/cable-ampacity.json (IEC 60364-5-52 Table B.52.4, method B1 conduit in wall or C clipped direct) with a minimum size per circuit (lighting 1.0 mm², other 2.5 mm² [ENGINEER]); the chosen cable is the smallest that also keeps `ΔV = 2 × L × I_b × ρ / A` (√3 × L × I × ρ / A three-phase) within 3% lighting / 5% other. Every cable rating is derated for air temperature and grouping (section W). Pakistani n/d sizes: the smallest whose real area is at least the IEC size (section W). Errors (not undersizing) when no listed cable or breaker fits. PF for the design current is an input; when it is blank, PF comes from the load type (the appliance's, or the one picked on the page) (data/defaults.json `pf_by_load_type`: inverter AC 0.9, non-inverter compressor 0.8 [ENGINEER]), else `pf_mixed` 0.8, so a 2 ton non-inverter AC gets a 20 A MCB, not 16 A (audit 2026-10-14, WL-10). Three-phase uses the three-loaded-conductor ratings.
- **MCB size (W3):** `I_n` as above; curve from load type (IEC 60898-1: B 3–5 × I_n, C 5–10 × I_n, D 10–20 × I_n); the matching cable is the smallest with `I_z ≥ max(I_b × margin, I_n)` (method input, default B1, derated as in section W) above the minimum size; PF as for W1/W2.

## Phase 4 tools (added 2026-10-13, Wave 2 pages and the Home Power Planner). Assumptions marked [ENGINEER] need engineer sign-off.
- **What a generator can run (C7):** the generator rule of section C solved for the load. `capacity_W = kVA × 1000 × PF` (PF 0.8); a load fits when `P_run × margin ≤ capacity_W` AND `P_surge ≤ capacity_W` (P_surge = running + the largest starting step, section C), so `max_running_W = capacity_W / margin` and `max_start_W = capacity_W`. How many of one appliance: `n = floor(min(capacity_W / (run × margin), (capacity_W − start) / run + 1))`, never below 0 (all n run, one of them starts). Same rule as C6, so a generator this page says runs an AC is never smaller than the size C6 recommends for it.
- **Solar for a tubewell / pump (D15):** pump input `P = HP × 745.7 / η_motor` (section A, η 0.8); `E_day = P × pumping hours / 1000`; array = the solar-system rule of D5/D8 (`kWp = max(E_day / yield, P / 1000 / PR)`, panels = ceil(kWp × 1000 / panel_W)); no battery; the solar pump drive (VFD) soft-starts the motor, so surge = running [ENGINEER]; drive size `≥ max(P × margin, installed_kWp × 1000 / dc_ac_ratio_max)` as a floor (the D5/D8 inverter rule); the drive is chosen by the motor's rated kW (`HP × 745.7 / 1000`) and nameplate full-load current, and its maximum DC input must exceed the string's cold Voc (the page says so; no Voc check in the engine yet).
- **Solar quote checker (N3) range from data:** `Rs_per_W = price / (kWp × 1000)` (section G). The typical range comes from `data/solar-prices.json` for the chosen type (on-grid / hybrid): the listed size's `low_rs / (kW × 1000)` and `high_rs / (kW × 1000)`; between two listed sizes the Rs/W of each end is interpolated linearly by kW; below the smallest or above the largest listed size the nearest end's Rs/W is used. Range in rupees = Rs/W × kWp × 1000. Verdict below / within / above; `vs_low_pct`, `vs_high_pct` = distance from each end. When any listed size the range rests on has only one source, `low_confidence` is true and the page prints a notice; a size whose only source gives a single price is excluded from the data. Prices are secondary sources (installer lists, blogs), dated in the file, refreshed monthly; hybrid ranges mix battery sizes, so the page asks the reader to compare battery kWh separately.
- **Solar system price (D14):** typical installed price = the N3 range (Rs/W, same interpolation) × kW; mid = average of low and high; panel-only cost = kW × 1000 × panel Rs/W (data/solar-prices.json `panel_rs_per_w`); with a monthly saving entered, simple payback years = price ÷ (saving × 12) at each end of the range (no degradation, inflation or financing; the page says so).
- **Units cost (E6, appliance pages):** `units` takes an optional `tariff` (Rs per unit) and returns `rs = units × tariff`.
- **FPA exemption (section F, 2026-10-14):** the monthly FPA/FCA is charged only to unprotected consumers; lifeline and protected consumers get `fpa_rs = 0` whatever is entered (NEPRA review decision on the March 2026 FCA, SRO 852(I)/2026; EV charging stations and prepaid consumers are also exempt but are not consumer categories of these pages). The per-unit add-on rates are echoed in the result (`fc_surcharge_per_unit`, `qta_per_unit`, `fpa_per_unit`). The latest notified FPA lives in data/tariff-pk-2026.json `addons_secondary.fpa_per_unit.latest` and is NOT applied by default (a blank field is 0) because it changes monthly; only the FPA page pre-fills it.
- **Flags as text:** boolean results print through the `yesno` format (`{{out.fits|yesno}}`), never as numbers.
- **Inverter error codes (N2):** no formula; `data/inverter-codes.json` (manufacturer manuals, one source per brand) is printed as tables and filtered in the browser by brand and code text.
- **Off-grid array (D3, 2026-10-14):** with `off_grid` and night hours set, the panels must run the day's load and refill the battery: `E_night = P_run × night_h / 1000`, `E_load = max(E_day, E_night)`, `E_array = (E_load − E_night) + E_night / (η_inv × η_charge)` (η_inv = the battery calculator's inverter efficiency, η_charge 0.95 LiFePO4 or 0.85 lead-acid, chosen by the DoD), then `kWp = max(E_array / yield, P_run / 1000 / PR)`. One night only; days of autonomy are the D1 battery page. Grid-tied and hybrid pages (no `off_grid`) keep `E_array = E_day`.
- **Home Power Planner (H1, home page):** one appliance list (rows as in Phase 3) runs through the chain, each step by the calculator that owns its formula: load (`P_run`, `P_surge`, `E_day`, `units_month = E_day × 30`) → inverter (section C: `P_run × margin`, kVA, surge; raised to `installed_kWp × 1000 / dc_ac_ratio_max` when the planned array would otherwise exceed the D5/D8 DC/AC limit) → battery for the backup load (`Ah = backup_W × backup_h / (V_bank × DoD × η_hybrid)`, section D; LiFePO4 DoD 0.9 or tubular 0.5) → solar (D8 rule from the same list and city) → net billing → generator (section C, whole list, next standard kVA).
  Solar size = the D8 rule `kWp = max(E_day / yield, P_run / 1000 / PR)` (both terms and the deciding one are shown). Net billing allows an array up to the sanctioned load, so the bill step uses `billed_panels = installed panels` when `installed_kWp ≤ sanctioned_kW`, else `floor(sanctioned_kW × 1000 / panel_W)`, and `billed_kWp = billed_panels × panel_W / 1000`; the planner then flags `solar_above_sanctioned` and shows a notice. Monthly solar split for the bill step: `solar_month = billed_kWp × yield × 30`; `self_used = min(solar_month, units_month × day_share)` (day_share = share of the month's use that happens while the sun is up, default 0.5, data/defaults.json `planner_day_share` [ENGINEER]); `export = solar_month − self_used`; `import = units_month − self_used`. Bill before = energy charge of `units_month` (section F, unprotected); bill after = net-billing energy charge of import and export (section F, buyback editable, not yet confirmed on a NEPRA page); `saving = max(0, before − after)`. Fixed charge, per-unit surcharges (FC, QTA, FPA) and GST are left out of both, so the saving is an energy-charge estimate; those items mostly fall too when fewer units are imported (GST is a share of the subtotal, the surcharges are per unit), so the real saving is usually somewhat higher. ≥ 5 kW sanctioned → ToU error as in section F. 

## Phase 5 tools (added 2026-10-14, Wave 3 pages). Assumptions marked [ENGINEER] need engineer sign-off.
- **Generator fuel (C8):** straight-line fuel curve (the form HOMER uses): `L/h = F0 × rated_kW + F1 × load_kW`, `rated_kW = kVA × PF` (pf_generator 0.8). F0 (no-load litres per hour per rated kW) and F1 (litres per kWh of output) per fuel live in data/generator-fuel.json with the points they were fitted from (petrol: Honda EU2200i and EU7000iS run time per tank at quarter and rated load, normalised per rated kW and averaged; diesel: the approximate diesel consumption chart) [ENGINEER]. Load above rated kW is an error. `L/day = L/h × hours`, `L/month = L/day × days` (30); cost = litres × fuel price (Rs/L, default the dated price in the same file, editable); `Rs per unit = Rs/h ÷ load_kW` and `L/kWh = L/h ÷ load_kW` (only when the load is above 0).
- **Solar panel price (D9):** `per panel = panel_W × Rs/W`, `total = per panel × panels` at the low and high end of data/solar-prices.json `panel_rs_per_w` (panel only, retail, dated); panels must be a whole number.
- **Solar output forecast (N6):** typical day `units = kWp × yield_city` (section D). With an hourly forecast of plane-of-array irradiance (W/m², hourly means, Open-Meteo `global_tilted_irradiance` at the city's coordinates, tilt data/defaults.json `forecast_tilt_deg`, facing south) fetched by the browser: `PSH = Σ GTI / 1000`, `units = kWp × PSH × PR` (PR 0.78), `pct_of_typical = units ÷ (kWp × yield_city) × 100`; sky label = first band of data/defaults.json `forecast_sky_bands` the day reaches. Each hourly value must be 0 to `forecast_max_wm2`; at most 48 values; the day's total `Σ GTI` (Wh/m²) must not exceed `forecast_max_day_whm2` (data/defaults.json, 12,000, [ENGINEER]), else `forecast_invalid`. The server page shows the typical day; the live forecast replaces it in the browser and is an estimate (forecast error, panel temperature and inverter clipping are not modelled).
- **Mosque and shop solar (N13):** load profile templates in data/load-profiles.json (appliance rows from data/appliances.json with hours per day [ENGINEER]) feed the D8 solar-from-loads rule unchanged (section D, Phase 3).
- **Peak hours (F13):** no new formula. ToU bill = section F `bill_tou`; the peak and off-peak windows by season are data (data/tariff-pk-2026.json `tou.peak_hours`, secondary until matched to the current DISCO schedule).
- **Data pages (Bill Index, Solar Price Tracker):** the Bill Index runs `bill_total` for each listed units level and billing month (data/bill-index.json gives the month's FPA and QTA period; every other rate from data/tariff-pk-2026.json); change = this month − last month, each total rounded to whole rupees first (bills are paid in whole rupees, so the change equals the difference of the totals shown), `change_pct = change ÷ last month × 100`. The tracker prints data/solar-prices.json (and data/solar-price-history.json for earlier months) with `Rs/W = Rs ÷ (kW × 1000)`; no other arithmetic.

## Engine contract and clarifications (Phase 1, 2026-10-13)
Each calculator is `engine/<file>.js` → `calc(inputs, data)` returning `{ ok: true, ...values }` or
`{ ok: false, error: code, field }`. `data = { defaults, tariff, cities }` from `data/*.json`. The `calc`
id is the one used in `calculator-test-cases.json`. Error codes map to user messages in `engine/messages.js`.
These rules fill gaps in the sections above; they are part of the formulas:
- **Rounding up** (panels, batteries, kW, standard sizes) ignores floating-point dust: 2.9999999 / 3.0000001 → 3.
- **Standard sizes** (panels, charge controllers, breakers) come from `data/defaults.json`; the result shows the next size up.
- **Battery bank count**: `series = V_bank / V_battery` (must be a whole number, else error), `strings = ceil(Ah_required / Ah_battery)`, `batteries = series × strings`.
- **UPS capacity** output key is `can_run` (`ok` is reserved for the result envelope). If one surge field is filled the other is required; both blank skips the surge check.
- **Standard sizes exceeded** (controller > 100 A): result carries `above_standard_sizes: true`; a wiring breaker above the largest standard size is the error `load_above_standard_breakers` (the unused `design_current` calculator was removed 2026-10-14, its cases now run on `breaker_size`) and the UI says to split the circuit/array or consult an engineer; a generator above the largest standard kVA gets its own notice (two generators or an industrial set, section C).
- **Loads list**: `start` defaults to `run`; `start < run` is an error; `qty` multiplies running watts; only the largest single start step is added for surge.
- **Sanctioned load**: recommended = max(min_sanctioned_kw, ceil(demand)); empty appliance list is an error; ToU warning when recommended ≥ `tou_threshold_kw` (5).
- **Bill, lifeline**: only when the user ticks lifeline; ≤ 100 units; all units at the lifeline band reached (1–50 → 3.95, 51–100 → 7.74), no fixed charge, no QTA. [VERIFY against a DISCO lifeline bill]
- **Bill, protected**: > 200 units is an error (protected cannot apply). **Bill, ≥ 5 kW**: must use the ToU calculation (`bill_tou`); the flat bill AND net billing refuse it. ToU fixed basis = max(`tou.fixed_min_fraction` (0.5) × sanctioned, MDI), from the tariff file.
- **Bill total**: subtotal = energy + fixed + FC surcharge × units + QTA × units + FPA × units; GST % applied to the subtotal. QTA/FPA may be negative (shown as credit lines); the subtotal is never below 0. Add-on rates are inputs; a blank field uses `tariff-pk-2026.json` `addons_secondary` (FC 3.23, GST 18, QTA for the chosen quarter `period`, FPA if notified else 0), each shown with its source and date. [VERIFY GST base and whether FC surcharge applies to lifeline]
- **Net billing**: if export credit > import energy charge, `net_energy_rs = 0` and the excess is `carry_forward_rs` (never a negative bill).
- **Bill audit (N1)**: slab jump = the 30-day-equivalent units fall in a LOWER unprotected band than billed units (a short cycle never flags). Fair estimate = all billed units × rate of the band the 30-day equivalent reaches + that band's fixed charge; `extra_rs = billed − fair` (min 0). Long cycle = days > 31. Spike = units > previous-year units × 1.5.
- **AC temperature (N9)**: saving capped at 100%; lowering the set point returns `extra_pct`/`extra_units`, never a negative saving. Set points limited to 16–30 °C.
- **Cleaning (N12)**: with a cleaning cost, `clean_after_days` = first day n where Σ_{k=1..n} kWp × yield × min(rate × k, cap) × tariff > cleaning cost (searched up to 365 days).
- **TCO (N7)**: `replacements = floor((years − ε) / life_years)`; fuel L/h table comes later from `data/generator-fuel.json`, until then energy cost per year is an input.
- **Autonomy (N14)**: if cloudy-day solar ≥ load, no day count is shown; the message says the solar covers the load.
- **Voltage drop**: if ΔV ≥ supply voltage the cable is rejected with a clear message (no negative voltage at load).
- **Inputs**: PF/efficiency/DoD must be 0.05–1, voltages ≥ 1 V; lifeline bills default FC surcharge and GST to 0 until verified (user can enter them); plain decimal text ("1500", "12.5") is accepted, also with thousands commas in groups of three or the Pakistani lakh grouping ("1,500", "12,000.5", "1,50,000"; audit 2026-10-14, WL-17), while any other comma ("1,5", "15,00") is an error, never read as a decimal point (hex/exponent text rejected), any value above 1e12 is rejected; yes/no ticks accept true/'true'/'on'/'yes'/1; blank uses the default; anything non-numeric, negative where not allowed, PF/efficiency/DoD outside (0, 1] is an error, never NaN.
- **Data in the engine**: `data = { defaults, tariff, cities, appliances, cables }`; `appliances` is data/appliances.json keyed by id, `cables` is data/cable-ampacity.json (loaded 2026-10-13 from IEC 60364-5-52 Table B.52.4; three-phase column B.52.5 and correction factors B.52.14 / B.52.17 added 2026-10-14; engineer to confirm).
- **Not yet in the engine**: N7 still takes the yearly energy cost as an input (the C8 fuel curve exists in `data/generator-fuel.json` and can feed it later).
