{{site.brand}} publishes a page only when its calculator works, its numbers come from the tested engine and a person has checked the text for accuracy and usefulness. This page explains how we write, review, update and correct content.

How pages are made

  • Each page answers one real question and has a working tool or original worked examples. We do not publish pages that exist only to rank for a keyword.
  • Numbers in the text, worked examples, tables and FAQ answers are generated by the calculation engine, not typed by hand.
  • Sources are linked on the page: NEPRA and DISCO notifications, Global Solar Atlas, IEC standards and manufacturer datasheets.

Use of AI tools

We use AI tools to help draft and code. Every page is reviewed by a person for accuracy and usefulness before it is published, and every formula is covered by automated tests. AI is never used to invent data, reviews or credentials.

Review and updates

Each page shows its author and the date it was last reviewed. Data that changes, such as tariffs and prices, is re-checked at least monthly.

Corrections

If you find an error, email {{site.contact_email}} with the page link. Confirmed errors are corrected on every page that uses the same formula or data, and the page's review date is updated.

Independence and advertising

Calculators do not favour any brand, and no advertiser, affiliate partner or installer can change a result, a default value or a recommendation.

Some pages have a "Where to buy" box with affiliate links. They are marked as sponsored links, the box carries a disclosure, and we may earn a small commission if you buy through them at no extra cost to you.

We do not use affiliate links yet. If we add them, they will sit in a labelled "Where to buy" box with a disclosure and be marked as sponsored links.

Solar pages may offer a free form to request installer quotes. Installers we pass requests to do not pay for, write or review any page content.

Advertising, when shown, is kept away from calculator inputs and buttons, is never placed on thank-you or error states, and is always labelled "Advertisement".
